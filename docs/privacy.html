Privacy Policy for Bolt PDF

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Bolt PDF (com.bolt.pdf) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Bolt PDF handles your files and optional Google Account data.

1. Local Files & Reading Data

By default, Bolt PDF operates offline on your device. Your PDF files, highlights, reading progress, and favorites are stored locally on your device.

2. Optional Google Drive Sync

If you choose to connect your Google Account in Bolt PDF Settings, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope.

  • What is accessed: Only the hidden, private app-specific folder (appDataFolder) inside your own Google Drive. Bolt PDF cannot see or access any other files in your Google Drive.
  • What is stored: Only the PDFs you choose to upload/sync, along with a sync manifest containing your reading progress (current page, total pages, zoom/rotation) and favorites status.
  • No external servers: Bolt PDF has no developer backend server. All synced data travels directly between your device and your Google Drive account.

3. Data Sharing & Limited Use

Bolt PDF does not sell, rent, share, or transfer your personal data or Google user data to third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers. Bolt PDF's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Deleting Your Data & Revoking Access

  • You can disconnect your Google Account at any time in Bolt PDF → Settings → Cloud Sync.
  • You can delete all synced app data and revoke access from your Google Account via Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Bolt PDF or Google Account Permissions.
